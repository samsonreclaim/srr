Manhole Raiser Rings

Precision Rubber Rings for Leveling Manhole Covers

Our rubber Manhole Raiser Rings are molded from dense, oil-resistant compounds that bring sunken manhole covers up to grade without the need for masonry work. Each ring delivers uniform seating, leak protection, and long service life in demanding roadway conditions.

Key Advantages:

  • Manufactured in resilient SBR / EPDM blends for excellent compression set resistance
  • Precision tolerances guarantee snug fit between frame and cover
  • Non-corrosive material withstands road salts, oils, and wastewater vapors
  • Lightweight rings reduce installation time compared to concrete collars
  • Custom branding or identification markings available on request

Typical Applications:

Roadway maintenance crews, utility service providers, municipalities, industrial estates, refineries, and water-treatment infrastructure that require quick adjustments to manhole elevations.

Technical Specifications:

Property Details
Base Material High-density SBR / EPDM rubber compound with optional fiber reinforcement
Inner Diameter Range 450 mm – 900 mm (custom machining available beyond range)
Raise Heights 6 mm, 12 mm, 18 mm, 25 mm, stacked configurations for higher requirements
Hardness 70 ±5 Shore A for superior load bearing without deformation
Operating Temperature -20 °C to 120 °C continuous service
Compliance Meets AASHTO / EN 124 seating tolerance guidance when installed per instructions

Installation Support:

Rings arrive cleaned, primed, and ready for placement. Skid-mounted kits with adhesive, shims, and installation instructions are available for city-wide maintenance programs.
